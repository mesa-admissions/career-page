The System

How It Works

Career prep at Mesa isn't one department — it's four interlocking systems that run across the full 12-month program. Each one compounds on the last.

Career Awareness & Discovery

Know what you're picking before you pick it: company immersion, domain primer and 1:1 mentorship from practitioners.

Shortlisting

Build the proof before you need it: resume, portfolio, sector theses, startup teardowns, AI hackathon builds, your own content channel, a video CV.

Interview Readiness

30+ problem-solving sessions, live data case presentations, themed demo days, and behavioural round prep.

Placement Readiness

Advanced Excel, SQL, Tableau, Python along with professional comms, tool mastery, and how to actually navigate a new org.

01 · System

Career Awareness & Discovery

Before you can pick a role, you need to know what the role actually is. Not just the job title.

01
Company Immersion

Company visits and operator sessions — see how startups really run, and what founders and CXOs look for.

02
Domain Primers

Fireside chats and "What Is" sessions on what each startup role actually involves, so you can pick the 2–3 you'll target.

03
1:1 Mentorship

Ongoing 1:1s with practitioners in your target roles — perspective before you apply, feedback after mocks.

04
Leadership Track

Strengths and personality assessments plus a career roadmap, so you know which roles and startups to prioritise.

02 · System

Shortlisting

Your shortlist odds are set long before an interview — in what's on the page. This is where Mesa builds that page.

The Container — Resume & Portfolio

Where everything you build gets packaged.

Resume — one page of quantified outcomes

V1 in Term 1, sharpened as applications approach. Ventures, projects, and real work experience lead over degree and certificates — and what leads shifts by target role.

Portfolio — proof before the offer

Sector theses, startup teardowns, AI builds, problem-based collateral, live project outputs — built progressively over the year.

The Evidence — Proof of Work

The core of the shortlist, built in sequence across the year. Each step produces a tangible, shareable artefact that goes on your profile.

01
Sector Deep-Dives

Analyse a sector and build a thesis, not just read one. 3 theses written and shared over the year, vetted by subject matter experts in the respective fields.

02
Startup Teardowns

Take apart any company: business model, what's working, what's broken, what you'd fix. Documented, then verified by startup operators.

03
AI Hackathon

Build 10+ AI agents, workflow automations, or lightweight products against real problems shared by operators, across 10+ hackathons through the year.

04
Build Your Own Channel

Grow a professional content channel like a founder would: startup teardowns, sector teardowns, new product launch/pitch and more.

Demo Day & mock interviews
03 · System

Interview Readiness

Shortlisting gets you the room. This is what gets you through it.

Problem Solving

Reps against real startup problems, judged by the people who set them.

1
Solve Startup Cases

Product-sense exercises and growth estimations — GTM, unit economics, growth loops, market entry, roadmap prioritisation and more.

2
Demystifying Startup Challenges

Solve real problems sourced directly from startups, then get your answer vetted by operators and founders.

3
Peer Venture Consulting

Consult fellow students on their business ideas and ventures, and create real immediate impact.

Themed Demo Days / Function Hackathons & AI Showcase

The live rounds — you build it, then you stand up and defend it.

01
Demo Days

Create a problem statement across Ops, AI, Growth, Product, Category, Sales and more. Go deeper, analyse, and build a solution MVP.

02
AI Showcase Day

Showcase AI agents and MVPs built during the term, and get reviewed by AI operators, mentors, and alumni.

03
Mock Interview Day

Function-specific mocks run back-to-back by potential recruiters themselves. Feedback given on the spot.

Assessment Formats

How you get measured — and what the room is actually grading.

Data Case Presentations (DCP)

Dataset in, insight out. Random groups present live each session, with feedback on the spot and the ideal approach shared right after.

Non-Data Assignment Presentation

Answer under ambiguity. Evaluated on how you frame the problem, prioritise, and present — not just the output.

Behavioural Prep

Culture round simulations and stress tests curated for your target companies and roles, then a negotiation workshop on evaluating an offer.

04 · System

Placement Readiness

Getting the offer is one thing. Being useful on Day 1 is another. This is the last mile — the operator toolkit that turns a new hire into someone a founder can hand real work to immediately.

Analytical Ability

Read the data, build the view, ship the answer.

Advanced Excel

Modelling, analysis, and decision-ready outputs under real constraints.

SQL

Pull and shape your own data instead of waiting on someone else.

Tableau

Turn raw numbers into dashboards operators actually use.

Python Basics

Automate the repetitive, and go beyond what spreadsheets can do.

Tool & AI Stack

The systems startups actually run on.

CRMs & Ops Tooling

Work inside the platforms teams use daily, so you're productive without a ramp-up week.

AI Agents & Automation

Build and deploy agents and workflow automations to move faster than the role expects.

Experimentation Frameworks

Design, run, and read experiments — the default way startups make decisions.

Rapid Prototyping

Ship lightweight products and MVPs on modern and AI-native stacks, without waiting on a dev team.

Professional Readiness

The unglamorous skills that decide how far you go once you're in.

Professional Communication

Crisp writing, executive-style storytelling, and stakeholder comms that land the first time.

Navigating a New Org

How startups actually operate: working with founders, managing async, and driving impact in your first 90 days.
